Strategic Framework: The 5-Phase Approach

A proven sequence that builds identity first, then amplifies it with paid advertising.

Phase 1: Define Identity (Weeks 1-2)

Before any content is created, we establish PMIS's unique brand positioning, core values, and differentiation narrative. This becomes the foundation for everything that follows.

Phase 2: Build Foundation (Weeks 2-3)

Consolidate social presence, claim Google Business Profile, establish owned web presence. This ensures PMIS is discoverable and credible when parents search.

Phase 3: Create Content (Weeks 3-4)

Develop strategic content calendar, train staff ambassadors, launch consistent identity-driven content. This builds organic reach and engagement without paid ads.

Phase 4: Amplify Reach (Weeks 4-8)

Execute content calendar, re-amplify earned media, build community engagement. Monitor metrics and optimize based on performance data.

Phase 5: Scale Growth (Week 8+)

Launch targeted paid advertising (Meta, Google Search) to scale successful campaigns. By this point, we have proven organic content that converts, so paid ads amplify winners, not experiments.

Operational Excellence: 6 Strategic Frameworks

Beyond creative content, we provide the systems and protocols that ensure consistent execution and measurable results.

1. Staff Ambassador Program

Teachers are your best brand ambassadors. We provide training, incentives (PD credits, recognition, stipends), and a strict media release protocol to ensure authentic, compliant content creation.

2. Content Templates & Language Strategy

Dual-language (English/Arabic) templates ensure consistency while respecting your diverse parent community. Clear guidelines on which content types run in which language prevent confusion and maximize reach.

3. Measurement Dashboard

A Looker Studio dashboard tracks all KPIs in real-time: social engagement, website traffic, lead generation, and enrollment conversions. Monthly reports show exactly what's working and where to adjust.

4. Crisis Management Protocol

Clear escalation matrix with named decision-owners at each level. Pre-drafted holding statements in English and Arabic ensure rapid, professional response to any online issue.

5. Enrollment Conversion Funnel

A 3-step funnel moves parents from ad engagement → lead capture → campus tour booking. Automated email nurture sequences (or manual fallback) keep leads warm and moving toward conversion.

6. Content Approval Workflow

Strict turnaround times (2-5 business days) and privacy checks ensure all content meets brand standards and complies with student media releases before posting.
