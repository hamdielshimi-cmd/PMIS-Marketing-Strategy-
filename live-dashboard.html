Enrollment Nurture Funnel Mockup

The automated sequence designed to move parents from initial interest to booking a tour.

📱

Step 1: Ad Engagement & Lead Capture

Parent clicks a Meta Ad (e.g., "Parent Testimonial Reel") and submits their email to download the "Choosing an International School" guide.

↓
📧

Step 2: Automated Nurture Sequence

Day 1: Guide Delivery & Welcome.
Day 3: Student Success Story (Proof).
Day 7: Invitation to Book a Tour.
Day 14: Urgent reminder (Limited spots).

↓
🏫

Step 3: Conversion (Campus Tour)

Parent books a tour via the automated link. The lead is tagged in the CRM, and the Admissions Team takes over for the in-person experience.
